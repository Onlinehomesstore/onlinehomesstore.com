# UNIK CARE. Website page structures (for content writers)

**Copy rules (strict):** Never use em dashes, en dashes, hyphens, or ampersands. Prefer “and”, a comma, or a short sentence.

**Core brand idea:** UNIK CARE makes beautiful hair colour easier to achieve at home.

## Homepage SEO (Admin → Online Store → Preferences)

- Title: UNIK CARE Hair Colour Shampoo and Hair Dye Gel
- Meta: Discover UNIK CARE Hair Colour Shampoo and Hair Dye Gel created for beautiful, natural looking colour and easy at home application. Explore our hair colour collection today.

**SEO:** Title `[Product Name] | UNIK CARE`. Meta: natural, not keyword stuffed. Alt text descriptive.

Removed forever from PDP: Why choose us, Your history / recently viewed, and the redundant footer upsell trust strip (footer newsletter remains global).

Do not invent ingredients, application times, ratings, reviews, or performance claims. Shopify product data provides the facts. Template provides the voice.
